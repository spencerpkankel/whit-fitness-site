Start here

Pick the program that sounds like you. Each one has a single first step, and none of them require you to be fit already.

CrossFit

For anyone who wants to get stronger and fitter in a coached group class.

Book an On-Ramp

  1. Book an On-Ramp

    $50 a session, or $60 for a couple.

  2. Learn the movements

    One to four sessions one-to-one with a coach.

  3. Join class

    Pick a membership and book classes in Wodify.

HYROX

For runners, racers and anyone who loves conditioning. No barbells, no On-Ramp needed.

Try a free class

  1. Pick a class

    Wednesdays, Saturday or Sunday morning.

  2. Come in free

    Your first class costs nothing.

  3. Choose a plan

    HYROX at $120 a month, or Unlimited at $160.

Vitality 55+

For adults 55 and over who want to stay strong, steady and independent.

Register for Vitality

  1. Register online

    Or call (458) 234-3673 and we'll help.

  2. Come to class

    Tuesday or Thursday at 10am with Coach Hannah.

Personal training

For anyone who wants a program built around their own goals, schedule or injuries.

Book a free consult

  1. Free consult

    Talk through your goals and get matched with a coach.

  2. BMW assessment

    InBody scan, movement assessment and bike test.

  3. Start training

    Your program arrives in the CoachRx app.

Youth

For middle and high school athletes. Run by ATP, our youth program.

Book a $20 drop-inAbout ATP

  1. Create an Upper Hand profile

    ATP classes book through Upper Hand, separate from gym memberships. Drop in for $20 or join for $120 a month.

  2. Train Monday to Thursday

    Sessions start at 6:30pm at Whit Fitness.

Already train somewhere else? Book a drop-in for $25 or a week pass for $60. Questions first? Check the FAQ or call (458) 234-3673.
