Questions

The things people ask before their first class.

All FAQs
Do I need to be fit or have experience to start?

No. Every program is built for beginners too. New CrossFit members start with On-Ramp: one to four one-to-one sessions with a coach ($50 each, or $60 for a couple) before joining class. HYROX and Vitality 55+ don't need an On-Ramp, and your first HYROX class is free.

Can I drop in for a class?

Yes. A single class is $25 and a full week pass is $60. Book on our class calendar in Wodify. If you haven't done CrossFit or functional fitness before, we'll ask you to do one On-Ramp session first.

What's the difference between CrossFit and HYROX classes?

CrossFit classes mix weightlifting, gymnastics and conditioning. HYROX classes focus on running, machines and sled work, with no barbells or advanced gymnastics. Unlimited members can do both.

How much is a membership?

Unlimited is $160 a month, couples are $270, HYROX (2 classes a week) is $120 and Open Gym is $100. See all pricing.

What should I bring?

Comfortable workout clothes, training shoes and a water bottle. The lounge has lockers, a shower and a changing room if you're heading to work after.

Is there childcare?

Yes, Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 9am. Kids are welcome at every class, and outside childcare times parents are responsible for their kids.

Do you offer student or military discounts?

Yes, for both. Call or text (458) 234-3673 and we'll set it up.
